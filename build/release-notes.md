# Chango 1.0

Chango is a drag-and-drop file converter and manager for Windows 10 and 11. Drag files, tap **Shift**, and let go on the format you want: the result appears next to the original, which is never changed. It's free, works on your own PC and has no limits or account.

## Download

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| **`Chango-Setup.exe`** | The installer. It installs for your account only (no administrator rights needed), adds Chango to the Start menu and keeps it up to date. |
| `Chango-Portable.zip` | No install: unzip it anywhere and run `Chango.exe`. |
| `SHA256SUMS.txt` | Checksums, to confirm your download is intact. |

Chango's downloads aren't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may say it "protected your PC". Choose **More info → Run anyway**.

The first time Chango starts, a short tour shows you the gesture and lets you try it on a sample file.

## What it does

- **Drag, tap, let go:** drag files, tap **Shift**, and let go on what you want. Tap **Shift** again to switch between Convert, Tools and Actions. No dragging? Press **Ctrl + Alt + Space**, or right-click files and choose **Chango**. Every result can be undone for a few seconds.
- **Documents:** Word to PDF, TXT, Markdown, HTML, RTF and ODT; Markdown and text to PDF, Word and HTML.
- **PDF:** to Word, PowerPoint (a slide per page), Excel (tables rebuilt from the layout), text and Markdown. Merge, split, compress and rotate.
- **Spreadsheets:** to PDF, XLSX, CSV, TSV and ODS. **Slides:** to PDF and images.
- **Images:** PNG, JPG, WebP, JFIF, BMP, TIFF, GIF and PDF, including from SVG, HEIC, AVIF and RAW. Edit, compress, resize, rotate and copy text out of images.
- **Video and audio:** MP4, WMV, GIF, MP3, M4A, WAV, WMA and FLAC; MKV, WebM, MOV, AVI, OGG, Opus and AIFF with FFmpeg installed. Trim, join, compress and normalize.
- **Subtitles:** SRT and VTT both ways, and a plain-text transcript.
- **CorelDRAW:** CDR and CMX to PDF, SVG and images.
- **Archives:** extract ZIP, 7Z, RAR, TAR, GZ, CAB and ISO; make ZIP, 7Z and TAR.GZ.
- **Make it yours:** hide and reorder slices in Settings → Wheel; light, dark or follow Windows.

## Privacy

Everything runs on your PC: no uploads, no account, no telemetry. The only network request is the update check, which asks GitHub every few hours whether a newer version exists and sends nothing about you or your files. Turn it off in Settings → About. Details: [privacy policy](https://github.com/tiwariramesh/Chango/blob/main/docs/PRIVACY.md).
