# Flipr release notes

## 1.0.0-beta

The first Flipr release, built on OrbitDrop 0.4.4 (MIT).

- **Documents:** Word to PDF, TXT, Markdown, HTML, RTF and ODT; Markdown and text to PDF, Word and HTML.
- **PDF:** to Word, PowerPoint (a slide per page), Excel (tables rebuilt from the layout), text and Markdown.
- **Spreadsheets:** to PDF, XLSX, CSV, TSV and ODS. **Slides:** to PDF and images.
- **Subtitles:** SRT and VTT both ways, and a plain-text transcript.
- **Media (with FFmpeg installed):** MKV, WebM, MOV, AVI, OGG, Opus and AIFF.
- **Archives:** extract 7Z, RAR, TAR.XZ, TAR.BZ2, CAB and ISO; make 7Z and TAR.GZ.
- **Its own look:** Flip Teal, a new icon, a redesigned tour, Settings, tray menu and wheel.
